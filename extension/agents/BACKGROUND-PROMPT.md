## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production panoramic background for a Thai desktop Chrome extension's AI sea-animal team status banner; NOT a mockup, no UI lettering, no animals.
Primary request: a premium believable underwater creative studio shared by a scholarly green sea turtle writer, a lavender octopus painter, a blue dolphin narrator and an orange crab video editor. Render ONLY their environment, leaving characters to be overlaid by the app.
Scene: inside an elegant cozy undersea atelier with a broad curved observation window into a subdued teal reef, rounded dark navy architecture, warm wood and understated brass details that match high-quality 3D collectible sea-animal figurines. On the far right show a carefully composed small creative workbench with a parchment/quill and closed books, an artist's palette and small canvas, a studio microphone with headphones, and a film reel with editing console. Four recognizable departments through understated props, not four separate panels.
Composition: ultra-wide panoramic image, 3:1 aspect ratio. Keep the LEFT 25% clear for an overlaid full-body character. Keep the CENTER 25–72% a quiet dark navy/teal wall/window with broad negative space for two lines of Thai text. Place interesting studio props and reef depth mainly in the RIGHT 25% and perimeter. The image will be cropped vertically into a low 124px banner, so all meaningful room details must be inside the middle horizontal 45% of the image; avoid important objects at top or bottom. Fixed slightly elevated eye-level camera, tasteful spatial depth, no fisheye.
Style: meticulously rendered cinematic 3D environment with soft satin/clay surfaces, realistic wood grain, subtle glass reflections, restrained physically believable materials, not flat SVG and not a neon game UI.
Lighting: warm amber desk light on the right, soft cool teal ocean fill, subtle underwater light rays; center must stay dark and uncluttered for readable white text. No bright lamps or high-contrast edges in central text area.
Constraints: no characters, no animals, no humans, no robots, no readable text, no logos, no watermarks, no border, no drawn UI controls, no extra floating icons, no busy particle effects. Opaque image.
